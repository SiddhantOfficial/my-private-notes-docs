Privacy Policy

Recurring Tasks & Issue Scheduler for Jira · Last updated: September 27, 2026

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge and carries the Runs on Atlassian designation. Your data never leaves Atlassian's infrastructure. We do not sell, share or analyse it.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Siddhant Sharma ("we", "us"). Contact: support@technicaldost.com.

2. Data the app stores

In Atlassian's Forge storage, inside your Jira site's data residency region:

  • Schedules: name, target project, recurrence rule, time zone, and the issue template you write (summary, description, labels, priority, parent key, sub-task names, options).
  • Atlassian account IDs: the schedule owner (used as reporter) and, if you choose one, a fixed assignee. No names, email addresses or other profile data.
  • Run history: time, result, created issue key and error text. Deleted automatically after 90 days.

3. How data is used

Only to create the Jira issues you scheduled and to show you their history. The app reads an issue only to check whether the previous occurrence is closed, or when you choose Repeat this issue.

4. What we do not do

  • No data is sent outside Atlassian (no egress, no external servers).
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • We, the developer, have no access to your data.

5. Personal data and closed accounts (GDPR)

The app uses Atlassian's personal data reporting API every week. When an Atlassian account is closed, its account ID is removed from every schedule: ownership is cleared and the assignee is unset.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a schedule deletes it and its run history immediately.
  • Run history expires after 90 days.
  • After uninstalling, Atlassian deletes all app data according to the Forge data retention policy.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of data at any time by emailing support@technicaldost.com. Jira admins can also delete schedules directly in the app.

8. Sub-processors

Atlassian (hosting and storage via Forge) is the only sub-processor.

9. Changes

We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
