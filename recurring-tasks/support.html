Frequently asked questions

Does it use my Jira Automation limits?

No. Scheduled issues are created by the app, so they don't count as Jira Automation rule executions.

What time are issues created?

Within about an hour after the scheduled local time. Schedules are checked hourly.

Can I schedule "the last Friday of every month"?

Yes: Monthly → "On a weekday" → Last + Fri.

What happens on 31st in shorter months?

Day 31 runs on the last day of shorter months. A yearly February 29 runs on February 28 in non-leap years.

Can people without admin rights use it?

Yes. Anyone with Create issues permission can schedule issues in that project.

Where is my data stored?

Only in Atlassian's Forge storage. See Privacy and Security.

How do I request a feature?

Email us. Feature requests from customers are prioritised.
